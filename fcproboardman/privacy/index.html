FC ProBoardman – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

FC ProBoardman is a single-player game. It has no accounts of its own, and the developer does not run any servers that receive your data.

Data stored on your device

Your saved careers, settings and any club names you edit or import are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. Name packs you import are read from a file you choose and are never uploaded.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes or hits an error, a crash report is sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics so the bug can be found and fixed. A report contains:

  • the error message and where in the game's code it happened
  • the game screen and in-game week at the time
  • your device model, operating system version and app version
  • a random installation identifier created by Crashlytics

Crash reports do not contain your saved careers, club or player names, or your advertising ID. They are used only to fix bugs and are kept for up to 90 days. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To see which parts of the game players enjoy and where they get stuck, the app sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase:

  • which game screens are opened, and events such as starting a career, finishing the tutorial or a season, completing a board challenge, watching a rewarded ad or buying Pro
  • the app language and whether Pro is active
  • your device model, operating system version, app version, country (from your IP address) and a random app-instance identifier

Statistics do not contain your saved careers or club and player names. The advertising ID is not collected for Analytics, and Analytics data is not used for advertising. Data is kept for up to 14 months. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Google Play Games: achievements, leaderboards and cloud saves

If you use Google Play Games, the game sends your achievements and leaderboard scores (trophies won, legacy points, best top-flight points and fanbase) to your Play Games profile. Your saved careers are also backed up to your own Google account (Play Games Saved Games) so you can restore them on another phone; the developer cannot read these backups. Leaderboard scores may be visible to other players as set in your Play Games profile settings. See policies.google.com/privacy.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device only (for example when building work is almost done). No data is sent for this. You can turn reminders off in Settings or in your device settings.

Optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional video ads in exchange for in-game perks. Ads are only requested when you choose to watch one. They are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • your IP address and approximate location
  • information about your device and how you interact with ads

This data is used to show, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. In the EU/EEA and UK you are asked for consent first; you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

See Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Players with Pro never see ads.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

The optional "Pro" upgrade is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the game only receives confirmation of whether you own Pro. See policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here before the change takes effect.
